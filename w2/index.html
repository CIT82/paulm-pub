Homebrew PC's for Enthusiasts

The first time I used a PC was in a highschool computer lab. I took a logo programming course with my dad after hours at Sierra High School. From the very begining I wanted to know more about what I could do with these magical boxes. The first computer my parents bought after that was an Apple IIc, which introduced me to perfirals and how you could expand the simple store bought computer to make it even better. In 1987 I talked my dad into helping me purchase and build my first homebrew PC, a 08386 based machine (with an 80387 FPU), 20MB hard drive, 5.25" & 3.5" floppy drives, sound blaster and a vga graphics card. I have built many computers over the years for myself, friends and family. I really enjoy the research, build process and tuning to get the most out of the hardware.
